scripted check of the shell, fonts and units. It was done for wayfinder ticket
[Identify the visual target from the existing figure corpus](https://github.com/mitchbradshaw/CNN/issues/9).

the per-frame evidence behind it. Frame names are the `.pen` top-level frame names.
